What it's like

More than a trip. A season that changes you.

You'll live with the people you serve, learn their rhythms and become part of a team already doing the work every day.

Real relationships

Children at the orphanage, inmates in prison ministry, families in the village. You'll know people by name, not just faces in photos.

Hands-on ministry

Teaching English, leading Bible studies, health outreach, building projects and literature work. You'll do real work that matters.

Growth you can feel

Daily worship, mentoring from experienced missionaries and the stretch of a new culture build a faith that's truly yours.

A typical day in the field

Every post is different, but most days follow a rhythm like this.

  1. 6:30

    Morning worship

    Personal devotions, then worship together as a team.

  2. 8:00

    Ministry work

    Teaching classes, helping at the orphanage, visiting families or working on a building project.

  3. 12:30

    Lunch together

    Simple, healthy, local food, often shared with the people you serve.

  4. 14:00

    Outreach & visits

    Bible studies, prison visits, health talks or literature distribution with local workers.

  5. 19:00

    Evening program

    Evangelistic meetings, youth activities or quiet study and rest.

Programs

Two ways to go.

Pick the one that fits where you are in life. You'll choose it on the first step of the application.

Ages 17+

Student Missionary

For students who are still in school or recently graduated and ready to give a summer, a semester or a year.

  • Short-term: a summer, a semester or one year
  • Great for a gap year or a break between studies
  • Under 18? A parent or guardian signs consent
  • No degree necessary
Apply as a student
Ages 18+

Long-Term Missionary

For adults out of school who sense a call to a longer season of full-time service overseas.

  • Commitments of one year or more
  • Singles, couples and families welcome
  • Professional skills put to work: medical, trades, teaching
  • No degree necessary in most cases
Apply for long-term service

How it works

From "I'm interested" to "I'm going."

No more printing, scanning and emailing PDFs. Everything starts online.

  1. Apply online

    Fill out one guided form in about 20 minutes. Your progress saves as you go.

  2. References

    We contact your pastor and two other references for you.

  3. Interview

    A friendly video or phone call to get to know you and talk about where you'd fit best.

  4. Placement

    We match you with a post and help you plan travel, visas and support raising.

  5. Go & serve

    Orientation, then you're on your way, with a team supporting you the whole time.

Guidelines

What we ask of every volunteer.

These standards keep you, your team and the people you serve safe, and keep the focus on Christ.

A living relationship with Jesus

Volunteers are committed Christians who take part in daily devotions, team worship and Sabbath services.

A healthy lifestyle

No alcohol, tobacco, vaping or recreational drugs while serving. Meals are typically vegetarian.

Modest dress & conduct

Dress and act with respect for local culture and Christian standards. Your field director will brief you on specifics.

Healthy relationships

Dating relationships are not started while in the field, and you must follow all child-protection policies without exception.

Teachable spirit

Follow the direction of your field director and local leaders, even when things are done differently from home.

Technology & social media

Never post photos of children or sensitive locations without permission. Some fields need extra discretion.

Health & travel readiness

A valid passport, required vaccinations, a physical check-up and travel insurance before departure.

Financial support

Most volunteers raise support for travel and living costs. We'll share practical ways to do it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a college degree or special training?

In most cases, no. We look for a willing heart, good character and a teachable spirit. Skills like teaching, nursing, construction, music or cooking are a bonus, not a requirement.

How old do I have to be?

Student missionaries must be at least 17. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian needs to give consent, which you can include right in the online application. Long-term missionaries must be 18 or older.

How long can I serve?

Students usually serve for a summer, a semester or a full school year. Long-term missionaries usually commit to a year or more. Tell us your availability on the application and we'll find what fits.

How much does it cost?

Costs depend on the country and the length of service. Volunteers usually cover airfare, insurance and personal expenses, and many raise support from family, friends and their church. We'll go over the numbers for your placement during your interview.

Can I choose where I go?

Yes, you can list your preferred countries. We do our best to honor them, but final placement depends on current needs and how your skills fit.

Is it safe?

Your safety is a priority. Every volunteer serves under an experienced field director, follows local guidance and has a support team at home. We'll go over safety plans for your specific location before you leave.

How long does the application process take?

The online form takes about 20 minutes. After that, references and an interview usually take 2–6 weeks. Apply at least 3–4 months before you hope to leave so there's time for visas and preparation.

Can I save my application and finish later?

Yes. Your progress saves automatically in your browser, so you can close the page and pick up where you left off on the same device.

I'd rather use the paper application. Is that still available?

Yes. The printable PDF applications are still on the Missions & Applications page. Online is faster, though, and we'll have your application right away.

Who can I talk to before applying?

We'd love to hear from you. Email office@lmn.org or call (208) 245-5388.

Ready to go?

It takes about 20 minutes, and you can save as you go.
